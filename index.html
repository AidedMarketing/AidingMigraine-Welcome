The diary

What it does

  • ●

    Log an episode in seconds. Start it with one tap, note your pain, and end it when it’s over.

  • ●

    See your patterns. Pain, duration, symptoms, triggers, and what came before and after — in one place, over weeks and months — and barometric pressure alongside your episodes, so you can see whether weather plays a part.

  • ●

    Bring it to your clinician. Export your history as a PDF, CSV, or JSON file to share at an appointment.

  • ●

    Lock it if you want. Optional on-device encryption with a passphrase you choose. If you forget the passphrase, it can’t be recovered — there’s no backdoor — so keep an unencrypted backup somewhere safe.

  • ●

    Gentle reminders. A daily check-in, and check-ins while an episode is running, if you turn them on.

What it never does

Quiet by design

  • ●

    No account, no sign-in, no streaks to break.

  • ●

    No ads, no feed, nothing that flashes or shouts.

  • ●

    No promises about your migraines. It’s a diary, not a treatment.

  • ●

    Skip a day and nothing is lost. It will be here when you come back.

Your data stays here

Privacy, in plain English

There is no account, no sign-in, and no third-party analytics. Your journal stays on your device by default. Only two optional features send anything anywhere, and only if you turn them on: notifications (to remind you) and weather (to show pressure changes alongside your episodes). If you send feedback, it goes by email to support@aidingmigraine.com — only what you choose to include.

One small thing about this page itself: if you set the light or the tone above, that choice is remembered in this browser, on your device only. It is never sent anywhere, and this page collects nothing.

Questions

Answered plainly

Is this medical advice?

No. Aiding Migraine is a personal diary. It can’t diagnose you or tell you what treatment or medication changes to make — only your clinician can do that.

Where does my data live?

On your device. There’s no account and no analytics. Only two optional features — notifications and weather — send anything, and only if you turn them on. And if you set the light or tone on this page, that choice stays in this browser, on your device.

What does testing involve?

Two weeks. Install it, log episodes as they happen — or don’t; skip days freely — then tell me what worked, what didn’t, and what’s missing. You can use the feedback page linked from the app, or just email me.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes — it runs in your browser and installs to your home screen. iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app / Add to Home screen.

What happens after the beta?

I’ll fix what testers found, then open it more widely. Your journal is yours either way — export it any time as PDF, CSV, or JSON.

A quick note

Aiding Migraine is a personal diary for tracking your migraines — it is not medical advice, and it can’t diagnose you or tell you what treatment or medication changes to make. Only your clinician can do that. If something feels sudden, severe, or different from your usual pattern, get urgent care and talk to your doctor.
