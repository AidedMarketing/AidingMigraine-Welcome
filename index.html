What it does

Log an episode in seconds

Start it with one tap, note your pain, and end it when it’s over.

See your patterns

Pain, duration, symptoms, triggers, and what came before and after — in one place, over weeks and months — and barometric pressure alongside your episodes, so you can see whether weather plays a part.

Bring it to your clinician

Export your history as a PDF, CSV, or JSON file to share at an appointment.

Lock it if you want

Optional on-device encryption with a passphrase you choose. If you forget the passphrase, it can’t be recovered — there’s no backdoor — so keep an unencrypted backup somewhere safe.

Gentle reminders

A daily check-in, and check-ins while an episode is running, if you turn them on.

Your privacy

There is no account, no sign-in, and no third-party analytics. Your journal stays on your device by default. Only two optional features send anything anywhere, and only if you turn them on: notifications (to remind you) and weather (to show pressure changes alongside your episodes). If you send feedback, it goes by email to support@aidingmigraine.com — only what you choose to include.

A quick note

Aiding Migraine is a personal diary for tracking your migraines — it is not medical advice, and it can’t diagnose you or tell you what treatment or medication changes to make. Only your clinician can do that. If something feels sudden, severe, or different from your usual pattern, get urgent care and talk to your doctor.
